a) Pros:
   1) early and continuous delivery
   2) stakeholder collaboration

   Cons:
   1) increased overhead
   2) lack of predictability

b) Scrum Master: Leads the Agile development team and relays employee information from the Product Owner. The        Scrum Master should be proficient at implementing the Agile Methodology, organizing and assigning tasks, and      managing each Scrum team.

   Product Owner: Manage the product backlog and ensure the company gains maximum value from the product.            Product owners should be skilled at maintaining a product backlog, collaborating with the Scrum Master, and       communicating the original vision for the product.

c) Sprint Planning: Discuss how much effort each item on the product backlog will take and how much work the team    can complete in the upcoming sprint.

   Daily stand-up: Discuss what everyone worked on yesterday and what they will work on today. Additionally,         discuss any blocks

   Sprint Review: Get feedback from project stakeholders, celebrate accomplishments

d) Story: A software system requirement that is expressed in a few short sentences.
   Epic: Captures a large body of work that can be broken down into several smaller stories.
   Task: A unit of work that cannot be described as an epic, story or bug. Should take less than a day.
   Bug: Problem that needs to be fixed and may be blocking a story or task.
